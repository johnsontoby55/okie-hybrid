The format

Lift. Run. Grind.

Every athlete takes on all three. Placings in each test add up to your overall score, so a well-rounded day beats one big performance.

Test 1 · Strength

Lift

A test of pure strength. Classic barbell work, no complicated lifts. Find out how strong you really are.

  • Workout revealed closer to the event
Test 2 · Endurance

Run

Running and machines. A test of your engine and how well you can hold a pace when it starts to hurt.

  • Workout revealed closer to the event
Test 3 · Mixed Modal

Grind

One grindy finisher that pulls it all together. Simple movements, tough to finish.

  • Workout revealed closer to the event
No high-skill movements. No barbell Olympic lifts, no muscle-ups, no handstands. Full workouts and movement standards will be released before the event.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need gymnastics or Olympic lifting skills?

No. There are no high-skill lifts or gymnastics movements. If you can lift, carry, push, pull and keep a pace, you're ready.

How is the overall winner decided?

Each test is ranked on its own. Add your three placings together, and the lowest total wins your division.

Who is hosting?

The Okie Hybrid Championships are hosted by OKC Hybrid as part of the Okie Fitness Expo.

Is this part of the Okie Fitness Expo?

Yes. The Hybrid Championships run alongside the Okie Fitness Expo, and competitors get expo access on event day.

How do age divisions work?

There are age divisions for 40–49, 50–59 and 60+. You'll enter your age on event day when you register. Individuals are placed by their own age; pairs are placed by the average of both partners' ages.

Which level should I pick?

New to competing or want a lighter day: Lite. Compete often and lift heavy: Pro.

When does registration close?

Registration closes Saturday, February 6, 2027 at 11:59 PM CT, one week before the event.

Can I get a refund?

No. All entry fees are non-refundable for any reason, including withdrawal, injury or illness. Registrations are non-transferable.

Can I switch partners or divisions after I register?

Partner and division changes are allowed up to one week before the event. Email kodastaff@gmail.com to make a change.

Can spectators attend?

Yes. Friends and family are welcome to watch from the Pavilion.
